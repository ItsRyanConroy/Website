FPV Drone

The Problem: Wanted a fully custom-built 5-inch freestyle quad rather than a pre-built RTF drone, to learn how the frame, flight-controller stack, motors, and digital video system actually integrate.

The Solution: Hand-selected and assembled each subsystem individually instead of using a bundled kit.

Breaking it Down:

  • GEPRC GEP-MK5 5" freestyle frame
  • DolphinRC F405 V3 60A flight controller / ESC stack
  • MEPS Neon 2207 1950KV motors
  • Walksnail Avatar HD Pro digital video system, dual antenna
  • RadioMaster RP1 V2 ELRS receiver

HV Indicator Board

The Problem: The Mines Formula FSAE team needs to know when the tractive battery system has reached high voltage levels, as handling hardware at high voltages can have serious implications. As such, the design was needed for safety and rules regulations determined by FSAE international.

The Solution: A Non-Opto Flyback Converter with UVLO

Breaking it Down:

  • LT8316 flyback controller drives an isolated output with no optocoupler needed for feedback
  • Sumida 11328-T078 transformer (8:1:1 turns ratio) fully isolates the LV indicator side from the HV bus
  • 900V-rated primary MOSFET handles the 432V nominal / 504V peak tractive bus
  • UVLO threshold turns the indicator LED on above 60V, off below 50V — per rule EV.5.7, fully hardwired with no software control
  • IP66/IP67-rated panel LED mounts through the battery container wall, visible from outside
